## 🧭 Overview

**Interactive Algebra Trainer** is a modern, lightweight web application designed to practice fundamental and advanced algebra topics. It combines structured theoretical guides, step-by-step examples, and interactive problem sets ranging from negative numbers and fractions to polynomials, roots, and systems of equations.

Powered by **KaTeX** for real-time mathematical typesetting.

---

## 📚 Covered Topics

The training modules include:
* ➕ **Negative Numbers** (arithmetic rules & operations)
* ✖️ **Column Multiplication & Division**
* ➗ **Common & Decimal Fractions** (operations & conversions)
* 🔤 **Literal Expressions & Polynomials** (simplification, grouping, brackets)
* ⚡ **Power Properties & Exponents**
* 🧮 **Special Multiplication Formulas (ФСУ)**
* 📐 **Algebraic Fractions & Radical Expressions (Roots)**
* 📈 **Linear & Nonlinear Equations & Systems**
* 📉 **Inequalities & Interval Method**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3
* **Math Rendering:** [KaTeX](https://katex.org/) for fast math typesetting
* **Architecture:** Modular data-driven structure (`data.js` + dynamic rendering via `app.js`)
